# Homer Directories

## Introduction

Homer Directories is a set of free reference guides published as web pages by
a blind author. Each gathers information that is real but scattered, and puts
it where a screen reader user can find it quickly.

The purpose is to save the reader the search: someone has already visited the
sources, checked the links, and written down what was found. Each guide is
one web page under headings, in alphabetical order, with appendixes for
browsing a second way. Nothing is behind a sign-in.

## The Guides

Fuller descriptions are in [Homer Resources][resources].

- [AudioVault][audiovault] — movies and television shows that have audio
  description.
- [Blind Authors][authors] — 59 writers who are blind or have low vision, and
  86 books they wrote on their own.
- [Blind Creators][creators] — 115 people who author books, develop apps, or
  make presentations.
- [Blind Developers][developers] — 32 software developers and the software
  each has built.
- [Blind Movers][movers] — travel, recreation, and tourism while blind or low
  vision.
- [Blind Presenters][presenters] — 36 leaders in podcasts, talks, video,
  broadcast, and film.
- [Tech Training][techtraining] — 1304 free presentations on technology and
  accessibility.
